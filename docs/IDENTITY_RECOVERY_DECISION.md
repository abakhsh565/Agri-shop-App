# Identity recovery — product decision

**Status:** PROPOSED. Awaiting the owner’s approval.
**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Scope:** Account access when the phone, SIM, PIN or Agri ID is unavailable.
**Implementation:** None in this change. This document is the approval gate; the app keeps its
current behaviour until the owner chooses an option.

> **This is not `docs/RECOVERY.md`.** That document is the database backup and restore runbook. This
> one is about a person getting back into their own account.

---

## 1. The problem, in plain terms

The product currently treats the phone number as the identity key. That is convenient and it is why
sign-in is one field, but it creates a single point of failure:

- a lost or replaced SIM means the old number may stop receiving codes;
- a person can forget which number they registered;
- an Agri ID identifies an account but must never become a password;
- a PIN is a device gate, not an account-recovery factor;
- an owner cannot be expected to resolve every employee’s access problem personally.

The question is not “how do we make phone login stronger?” It is **“what independent proof can
reopen the same account without creating a duplicate identity or bypassing authorization?”**

---

## 2. What exists today (measured, not assumed)

Read-only checks on the local development stack on 2026-11-02:

| Fact | Measurement | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | 7 users | Existing IDs, phones and memberships must be preserved |
| Recovery keys issued | **0 of 7** | The key path exists but nobody has enrolled; it cannot be the only answer today |
| Accounts with a server PIN hash | 3 | A PIN is not universal and is not a recovery credential |
| Agri IDs | One per user, unique | A stable locator, not a secret |
| Verified email | **No email column or verification flow** | Email cannot be a recovery factor yet |
| Self-request access | Retired by migration 0288 | Onboarding is owner-managed; support must fit that model |
| Recovery-key code | `rotate_my_recovery_key`, `verify_recovery_identity`, `get_my_identity`, `AccountRecoveryPage` | Partial infrastructure exists, but key recovery currently resumes through the demo auth bridge and reports `unsupported` in SMS mode |

The partial key implementation is important: it proves the identity model can be kept, but it is not
a production-ready recovery process. **No key has ever been issued, and the live-SMS path is not
enabled.** The current `AccountRecoveryPage` also combines key verification with a replacement-phone
OTP, which means “I lost my SIM” still needs a working number unless support intervenes.

The security rules that must survive every option:

1. Reopen the **same** `users.id`; never create a second account for the same person.
2. Preserve Agri ID, phone history, memberships, roles, branch scope, permissions and business
   records.
3. The Agri ID alone is never sufficient.
4. A PIN is never disclosed, exported or reset by support; the person sets a new PIN on their device.
5. Every recovery is audited: who asked, what proof was checked, who approved, what changed.
6. Recovery must not grant a shop, role or branch that the membership did not already carry.
7. An employee’s recovery must not let them take over an owner’s account, or the reverse.

## 3. Options

### A. Recovery key — the independent proof

A high-entropy key generated by the server, shown once, stored only as a bcrypt hash (plus a lookup
hash), rotatable from the Identity screen.

**Strengths:** independent of SIM and email; works when both the phone and Agri ID are forgotten; can
be required for owners and Co-Owners; already has server primitives and a lockout counter.

**Limits:** 0/7 adoption; the current live-SMS path is incomplete; people lose paper; a stolen key is
a credential and must be revocable; a key alone should still land the person through a new phone
verification before opening a session.

**Recommendation:** make this the **primary voluntary proof**, mandatory at owner/Co-Owner onboarding
only after a migration/activation period. Keep it optional for employees, who can also be re-invited
by their owner.

### B. Verified email — a secondary factor

An email address the account has proved it controls, used only to deliver a recovery challenge.

**Strengths:** familiar; survives a lost SIM; useful for owners who travel.

**Limits:** the schema has no email column; deliverability and support cost; email accounts are also
lost/compromised; adding email changes the product’s identity surface and requires verification,
privacy notices and a bounce/fraud process.

**Recommendation:** **defer**. Do not add an email field just to satisfy a hypothetical case. Revisit
after the recovery-key flow is live and measured. If added, it is optional and never sufficient by
itself.

### C. Alternative identity — Agri ID, owner attestation, documents

Use the Agri ID to locate the account, then require evidence that the person is that account holder.
For employees, an owner/manager can attest to the person and the invitation; for owners, business
registration or in-person verification may be appropriate.

**Strengths:** matches how shops actually work; no new secret to lose; preserves the user ID.

**Limits:** Agri IDs are shareable by design; owner attestation is only as trustworthy as the owner;
documents create a privacy/retention burden; a compromised owner account could attest falsely.

**Recommendation:** use **only as part of a manual, audited support decision**. Never as a
self-service login. An employee can be re-invited by an authorized owner, but only after the existing
identity is proven or the old membership is explicitly closed and the history is retained.

### D. Forgotten phone number plus Agri ID

The person knows their Agri ID but not the number. The Agri ID can locate the account; the recovery
key can prove it. Without a key, the case goes to support.

**Recommendation:** support path, not self-service. If a key exists, self-service is acceptable:
Agri ID (optional locator) + recovery key + new-number OTP. If no key exists, a support agent must
verify identity and record the decision. **Agri ID alone must not reveal the registered phone number**
beyond the minimum needed to send a challenge.

### E. Lost SIM or replacement phone

Two different cases:

1. **Same number restored by the carrier.** OTP to the restored number is the normal proof. This is
   not an exception and should remain self-service.
2. **New number, no access to the old one.** The old phone cannot receive a code. The person needs a
   recovery key or manual support. A support-led change must:
   - prove the old identity (key, documents, owner attestation as applicable);
   - write the new number to the same `users.id`;
   - revoke existing sessions and require a new PIN on the device;
   - keep the old phone in history/audit, not as an active credential;
   - never create a second account because the new number is “free”.

**Recommendation:** same-number restoration is self-service; new-number replacement is key-first,
support-second. The existing `0283_profile_phone_change` path already keeps the identity; it must be
used rather than an account merge.

### F. Manual support recovery — the last resort

A documented human process for owners/Co-Owners (and employees whose owner cannot be reached):

1. Open a ticket with the Agri ID, the old number (if remembered), the new number and the reason.
2. Verify the caller against at least two independent pieces of evidence.
3. Require a recovery key when one exists; never silently bypass it.
4. For an owner, require business-identity evidence and a second reviewer.
5. Apply a cool-down (for example 24 hours) before a high-risk phone change or key reset.
6. Revoke sessions, require a fresh OTP and a new PIN.
7. Record the evidence, reviewer, decision and the exact rows changed in an immutable audit event.
8. Never read, set or reveal a PIN. Never delete memberships or business history.

**Recommendation:** allow support only with **two-person approval, evidence, cool-down and audit**.
Support cannot “look up the PIN”, cannot bypass an active recovery lockout, and cannot move an
employee into a different shop/role.

## 4. Recommended policy for approval

| Situation | Self-service? | Required proof | Identity outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Known phone, working SIM | Yes | OTP to that phone | Same user, normal session |
| Same number restored by carrier | Yes | OTP to that number | Same user, normal session |
| Key held, phone/SIM/Agri ID unavailable | Yes, once live | Recovery key + new-number OTP | Same user, new number, new PIN |
| Key lost, phone working | Yes | OTP + signed-in identity screen rotates key | Same user, new key |
| Phone + Agri ID forgotten, key held | Yes | Recovery key (key alone locates account) + new-number OTP | Same user |
| Phone + Agri ID forgotten, no key | **No** | Manual support evidence | Same user, support-led number change |
| New number after lost SIM, no key | **No** | Manual support evidence, owner/business attestation | Same user, new number, sessions revoked |
| Employee cannot reach owner | **No** | Manual support, employment evidence | Same user; membership unchanged; no new shop/role |
| Suspected key theft | Yes (signed in) or support | Rotate key, revoke sessions | Old key invalid immediately |
| Deactivated/rejected account | **No** | Existing account lifecycle decision | No recovery that bypasses deactivation |

**Decision requested from the owner:**

1. Approve the recovery key as the primary independent proof and set an enrolment deadline for
   owners/Co-Owners.
2. Approve “Agri ID is a locator, never a credential”.
3. Approve same-number SIM restoration as self-service, and new-number changes as key-first,
   support-second.
4. Approve manual support only with two-person approval, evidence, cool-down and audit.
5. Decide whether verified email is in scope for the next phase or deferred.
6. Name the support owner and the evidence standard for owner/employee cases.

## 5. Acceptance criteria for a future implementation

A future change is not complete unless all of these are measured:

- [ ] A recovery reopens the **same** `users.id`; the old and new phone resolve to one account.
- [ ] No duplicate user, membership, Agri ID or auth identity is created.
- [ ] The Agri ID alone cannot open a session, reveal the phone, or reset a PIN.
- [ ] Recovery-key values are stored only as hashes, shown once, and old keys are invalid after
      rotation.
- [ ] Five wrong keys lock the path for 15 minutes; the lockout is server-side.
- [ ] A successful recovery requires a new phone OTP and a new device PIN.
- [ ] Existing sessions are revoked after a number change or suspected compromise.
- [ ] Memberships, roles, branch scope, permissions and business history are unchanged.
- [ ] Manual support is two-person, evidence-backed, time-delayed and audited; support never sees a
      PIN.
- [ ] An employee cannot recover into an owner/Co-Owner account, and an owner cannot use an
      employee’s recovery to bypass owner authorization.
- [ ] EN + UR / LTR + RTL copy exists for every new state; no dead-end screen.
- [ ] The path is tested on a real browser against the local Supabase stack, then staging, before any
      production release.

---

## 6. Non-goals for this decision

- No account merge or “new account with old history” workaround.
- No recovery by Agri ID alone.
- No support-visible PINs or shared PINs.
- No deletion of historical memberships, audit rows or business records.
- No production data or production account changes in this phase.
- No implementation until the owner approves this policy.

---

## 7. Evidence and open risks

**Evidence used:** local development database read-only counts (7 users, 0 recovery keys, 3 PIN
hashes, no email column); `0286_one_identity_agri_id_recovery_and_explicit_invitations.sql`;
`src/lib/auth.ts` (`rotateMyRecoveryKey`, `recoverWithKey`); `AccountRecoveryPage`; `IdentityPage`;
and the removal of self-request onboarding in `0288_retire_employee_self_request.sql`.

**Open risks:**

- A recovery key is still a bearer secret. Theft is possible; rotation and session revocation must be
  fast and obvious.
- Manual support is the highest-risk path. Without a second reviewer and evidence standard it becomes
  a social-engineering door.
- The current key flow is demo-mode-bound; enabling it in SMS mode is a separate implementation task.
- Email recovery adds personal data and deliverability support; it should not be smuggled in as a
  “quick fix”.
- Owner attestation for employees is useful but not independent proof; it needs boundaries.

**Next step:** owner decides the six questions in §4. Only then should a migration/UI plan be written.
